The Nether is a formless plane of mutable magical essence. Its native beings, commonly called demons by mortals, do not naturally require fixed bodies and may exist as shifting presences, patterns, or raw magical forms.

Summoning forces or invites a Nether-being into physical manifestation. Names, circles, bindings, contracts, and similar rites stabilize that manifestation and may also compel it. Forced binding can amount to captivity from the entity’s perspective; pacts and voluntary manifestations operate through agreed terms rather than simple obedience.

The Nether is dangerous but not inherently evil. Its inhabitants are intelligences with their own motives, loyalties, rivalries, and ethics. Mortal ideas such as body, species, death, and identity do not always apply cleanly to them.
